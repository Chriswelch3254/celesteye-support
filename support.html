CelestEye support

Set your sky

Choose Use my sky to use your current location, or open Observing settings to enter coordinates or choose a city. Negative latitude is south; negative longitude is west.

Pointing looks wrong

Return to Now, update your current location, and move away from magnets, cars, and metal. Check the map against a known star. Use the heading and tilt correction controls if needed. You can always stop tracking and drag the sky manually.

Find an object

Open Find and search for a name or designation. Every official constellation remains searchable even below your horizon. Show in sky centers the map on supported objects; some moons, dwarf planets, and minor bodies contain reference information without a calculated sky position.

Offline use

Stars, constellations, packaged solar-system entries, sky calculations, manual locations, and time controls work offline. Additional JPL reference searches and StoreKit purchases require connectivity.
