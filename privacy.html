CelestEye privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

CelestEye does not require an account and includes no advertising or analytics SDKs. The developer does not operate a service that collects your app usage or observing location.

Location and motion

If you grant access, location and motion readings are used on your iPhone to calculate and align the sky. Your chosen observing place name, coordinates, source and civil time zone are saved on your iPhone. When you request current location, Apple may resolve its name and time zone using the coordinates. If that service is unavailable, you can use the offline city list or enter a time zone; unresolved times are explicitly labeled UTC. If you explicitly submit a city search, only the typed city query is sent to Apple’s geocoder, without your GPS location. Coordinates are not sent in NASA/JPL catalog searches. You can deny these permissions and use manual coordinates and sky navigation.

Optional Camera AR

Camera AR uses the rear camera on your iPhone to place sky labels over the live view. Live Camera AR does not record images. Optional saved-horizon scanning captures frames and camera/heading information on your iPhone to build a panorama for your manual skyline review. Temporary frames are discarded after review or cancellation; the panorama is retained only if you choose. You can delete it separately from the angular outline. Horizons and images are excluded from private iCloud sync, personal archives and device backups. A deliberate angular-profile export includes the saved coordinates, name, dates, calibration and outline, without imagery. CelestEye does not upload these images or profiles. You can deny camera access and continue using the sky map. Motion permission recovery may request Motion & Fitness authorization; CelestEye does not save or upload fitness or activity data.

Event reminders

If you choose Remind me for an event, CelestEye asks for notification permission and schedules a local notification on your iPhone. The reminder uses the observing location selected when it was created. There is no developer push-notification server, and your observing location and reminder choices are not uploaded. Changing observing place does not silently reschedule reminders; the app shows their earlier place and offers an explicit update. You can remove a reminder in the event details or change notification access in iPhone Settings.

Optional internet features

The packaged sky catalog and calculations work offline. If you request an additional asteroid or comet from NASA/JPL, the object name or designation and ordinary network information such as your IP address are transmitted to NASA/JPL. Up to 20 responses are cached on your iPhone. NASA/JPL controls its own service and privacy practices. Source links open external websites under their respective privacy policies.

Opening an aurora screen can download the global NOAA SWPC aurora model while that screen is active. Satellite planning can download current orbital elements for the supported satellites from CelesTrak. These requests send ordinary network information, including your IP address and the requested product or satellite identifier, to the provider. Your observing coordinates are used locally and are not included in these requests. Downloaded forecasts retain their original source times and expiry; they are not live sightings. There is no CelestEye monitoring server or paid weather service.

Optional private iCloud sync

Sync is off until you deliberately enable it. It sends saved targets, observing lists, viewing equipment, saved places, Journal entries and challenge learning/progress records to your private CloudKit database in your Apple account. This includes private notes, precise saved coordinates and Journal place snapshots. Apple operates iCloud under its own terms and privacy practices. CelestEye does not use a public database for these records. Turning sync off keeps the local records and leaves the existing iCloud copy in place. You can manage iCloud storage in iPhone Settings. Recent browsing, active viewing setup, display preferences, alignment, diagnostics, horizon profiles and panoramas are excluded. Availability depends on your account, storage and Apple services.

Widgets and Shortcuts

Widgets read a small local snapshot shared only with the CelestEye widget extension. It contains target/event names, viewing method, time zone, viewing times and source status. Place names are hidden by default and can be enabled in observing settings. Precise coordinates, Journal entries, notes and panoramas are not shared with the widget. Widget information can be visible on your Home or Lock Screen; Apple controls refresh timing. Focused Shortcuts actions use a small local index of supported selected or saved target names and identifiers. They do not provide Journal notes or coordinates to the action.

Exports and sharing

You choose whether and where to export or share. A personal archive includes private records, notes and saved places, but excludes horizon images and profiles. A complete offline plan can include its precise observing place, equipment, viewing windows and source snapshots. An angular horizon export includes its saved coordinates and calibration but no image. Observing cards and calendar exports expose only the fields shown in their preview and selected privacy options. Exported files are independent copies; deleting a record in CelestEye does not delete a file you already shared. The destination you select controls that copy.

Optional support purchases

Apple handles voluntary one-time support purchases. CelestEye receives StoreKit transaction information to verify and finish a purchase. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Purchases do not unlock features.

Local data

An optional, deliberate tracking snapshot stores a single aiming direction, roll, sensor timestamps, app version and thermal state locally. It contains no geographic coordinates or camera image and is shared only at your request. Saved targets, recent history, observing places, Journal records, lists, viewing setups, learning progress, saved event/reminder context and cached references use the app’s local storage. Private records also go to iCloud only when you enable sync, and exports go to destinations you choose. Deleting the app removes this local storage; device backups are governed by your Apple settings.
